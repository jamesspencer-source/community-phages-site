Instructor information

Instructors help students learn bench technique, read experimental results, troubleshoot phage work, analyze sequence data, and prepare final posters.

Weekly teaching focus

  • Early weeks: safety, pipetting, sterile technique, environmental samples, bacterial lawns, and plaque screening.
  • Middle weeks: purification, titers, lysates, microscopy, DNA extraction, host range, and data interpretation.
  • Late weeks: genome analysis, figures, poster practice, and final presentations.

Public reference materials

Use the public protocol, bioinformatics, glossary, notebook, and schedule pages as references for teaching and student support.
