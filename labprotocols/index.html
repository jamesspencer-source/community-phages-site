Lab Protocols

A sequence of protocols for sampling, plating, purification, lysates, DNA work, host range, and phage characterization.

  1. 01 · Project introduction The host strains, the scientific question, and why corynephages are useful.
  2. 02 · Pipetting exercises Practice with volumes, technique, and accuracy before plaque and titer experiments begin.
  3. 03 · Basic techniques Core microbiology practices for sterile work, bacteria, media, and safe bench habits.
  4. 04 · Acquiring and processing environmental samples Collect, label, filter, and prepare environmental samples for screening.
  5. 05 · Direct plating of environmental samples First-pass plaque hunting on host bacterial lawns.
  6. 06 · Phage enrichment Use host bacteria to enrich samples before plaque screening.
  7. 07 · Phage purification Use repeated plaque purification to move toward a clean isolate.
  8. 08 · Preparing high titer lysates for DNA extraction Prepare enough phage stock for DNA extraction and other assays.
  9. 09 · Extracting phage genomic DNA Preparing DNA from high-titer lysates for digest and sequencing work.
  10. 10 · Restriction digest Checking phage DNA and comparing digest patterns.
  11. 11 · Phage killing experiment Testing how phages affect bacterial growth across conditions.
  12. 12 · Host range experiments Comparing which bacterial strains are sensitive or resistant to each phage.
