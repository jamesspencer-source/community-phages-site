A program built year by year.

Community Phages began as a discovery-based summer program and now includes an 8-week RCC-HMS internship, instructor teams, PhagesDB records, partner visits, and student poster sessions.

Meet the team Resources

Program history.

The program has grown through student cohorts, instructor teams, partner visits, phage records, and poster sessions.

2022

Pilot year

The program began as a discovery-based summer research experience inspired by SEA-PHAGES and adapted for RCC students working at HMS.

View 2022 roster
2023

More instructors

The teaching team broadened across HMS labs, adding more phage defense, microbial evolution, and microscopy expertise.

View 2023 roster
2024

More public materials

Protocols, year-specific resources, instructor rotation, and student materials became more formalized.

View 2024 roster
2025

Public phage collection

Student discoveries were prepared for PhagesDB records, and poster-session practices became a stronger capstone.

2026

Current planning cycle

The program continues as an 8-week May-July research internship with field sampling, bioinformatics, partner visits, and a final poster session.

Diversity and equity mission.

Community Phages is designed to make an HMS research experience accessible to RCC students through paid time in the lab, close mentoring, clear protocols, and a teaching team that expects students to contribute real data.

Acknowledgments

The program was inspired by HHMI SEA-PHAGES, with protocols and materials adapted or reproduced with permission. HMS, RCC, sponsors, field-site partners, and mentors keep the summer running.
