Community Phages history.

Community Phages began as a discovery-based summer research program for RCC students at Harvard Medical School. This page lists prior instructor teams, program years, acknowledgments, and manual pages.

Meet the team Resources

Program history.

Prior years include instructor teams, field sampling, phage records, and poster sessions.

2022

Pilot year

The program launched as a paid summer research experience for RCC students working in an HMS microbiology lab.

View 2022 roster
2023

More instructors

The teaching team broadened across HMS labs, adding expertise in phage defense, microbial evolution, and microscopy.

Expanded manual

Protocols, year-specific resources, and student materials were expanded.

2025

Public phage collection

Student discoveries were prepared for PhagesDB records, with final posters as the capstone.

View 2025 roster
2026

Current program

The 2026 program runs May 18-July 9 as an 8-week research internship with field sampling, bioinformatics, partner visits, and a final poster session.

View 2026 roster

Diversity and equity mission.

Community Phages provides paid research time at HMS for RCC students. Students learn lab methods, collect data from their own samples, and present their results.

Acknowledgments

The program was inspired by HHMI SEA-PHAGES, with protocols and materials adapted or reproduced with permission. HMS, RCC, sponsors, field-site partners, and instructors contribute to the summer program.
