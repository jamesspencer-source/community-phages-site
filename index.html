An 8-week phage discovery program for RCC students at HMS.

Community Phages gives Roxbury Community College students paid summer research time in an HMS lab. Students collect environmental samples, test them for bacteriophages that infect corynebacteria, and work with instructors as the results come in.

Students and instructors.

8-week lab schedule.

The 2026 program runs May 18-July 9. Students start with sampling and bench skills, then move through plaque work, lysates, microscopy, DNA, genome analysis, and final posters.

W1 Bootcamp and sampling

Safety, pipetting, sterile technique, host bacteria, lab notebooks, and the first environmental samples.

Sample
W2 Plaque hunting

Filtered samples, direct plating, enrichments, bacterial lawns, and first evidence of phages.

Plaques
W3 Purify and titer

Students pick plaques, purify isolates, calculate titers, and prepare lysates for DNA work and characterization.

Purify
W4 Image and extract DNA

Electron microscopy, DNA extraction, restriction digests, and scientific meeting exposure.
